# Diorama Block Art

Medium-shot of Edward Elric from Fullmetal Alchemist, rendered as a physical stop-motion claymation figure made of square pixel blocks. He is kneeling under a large, gnarled tree on a grassy hill, gazing at a vibrant orange and yellow pixelated sunrise. His red auto-mail arm has a palpable clay texture. The sky is a gradient of blue to gold with pixelated clouds. Warm cinematic lighting, shallow depth of field, handcrafted diorama feel, 8-bit aesthetic, photorealistic texture on low-resolution geometry
A silhouette of Alphonse Elric's large armor from Fullmetal Alchemist, built from coarse, textured pixel bricks, sitting under a massive stylized tree. He is looking at a powerful, glowing sunrise breaking over a distant horizon. The sun casts long, defined pixelated shadows. The morning mist is made of translucent pixel art layers. Golden hour lighting, backlighting, highly detailed texture on pixel blocks, 16-bit JRPG style, stop-motion diorama photography.
Wide shot of a character resembling Edward Elric (red cloak, blond hair) constructed from miniature pixel blocks, looking out at a scenic sunrise. He is positioned beneath an old tree. The entire scene (tree, grass, rocks) is built from various textured pixel-shaped materials like wood and felt, photographed as a physical stop-motion set. Dramatic rim lighting from the rising sun, soft focus background, handmade craft aesthetic, retro gaming made real.
